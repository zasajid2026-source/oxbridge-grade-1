Lesson Plan

5.1 — Our health — keeping ourselves healthy
Unit 5 — Our health · Book page 42

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why we should care for our body.
  • Show how we should do to keep ourselves healthy.
  • Identify the things which can harm our body.

Introduction

Ask: Health is a great gift. How do we keep our body neat and clean?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book: Health is one of the greatest blessings of Allah. Health is the power of our body.
  • Say: Health is the complete well-being of body, mind, feelings, spirit and relationship with others. We should keep our body neat and clean.
  • Teach the book's list, one by one: Wash our hands. Take a bath daily. Take a balanced diet. Exercise daily.
  • Finish the list: Keep our nails short. Brush our teeth twice a day. Sleep and wake up at the proper time. Drink plenty of water.

Board Work

Keep healthy: wash hands, bath daily, balanced diet, exercise daily, nails short, brush teeth twice a day, sleep on time, drink plenty of water.

Class Activity

Children mime each healthy habit from the book's list as the teacher reads it: washing hands, bathing, eating, exercising, brushing teeth, sleeping, drinking water.

Assessment

  • Name three things we should do to keep ourselves healthy.
  • How many times a day should we brush our teeth?

Homework

Write any four healthy habits from the book's list.

Conclusion

Health is the power of our body. We keep ourselves healthy with clean habits every day.
