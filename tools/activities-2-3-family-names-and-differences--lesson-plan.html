Lesson Plan
4.3 — Activities 2–3 — Family names; old and young, tall and short
Unit 4 — Me and my family · Book page 36

Learning Objectives

  • Know about the members of a family.
  • Identify the similarities and differences of people.
  • Identify the names of some common body parts.

Introduction

Ask: Can you fill in the name of each family member? Who is old and who is young?

Teaching Steps

  • Open the book at Activity 2. Children fill in the blanks with the correct family names: mother, brother, grandmother, father, grandfather.
  • Move to Activity 3. Read the first pair: Which one is a young man? Which one is a young woman?
  • Read: Which one is old? Which one is the youngest?
  • Read: Which one is tall? Which one is the shortest? Children point and answer for the pictures.

Board Work

Activity 2 names: mother, brother, grandmother, father, grandfather. Activity 3: young/old, tall/shortest.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activities 2 and 3. Children fill family names in the blanks, then answer which person is old or youngest, tall or shortest, a young man or a young woman.

Assessment

  • Which family names are used in Activity 2?
  • In Activity 3, which one is tall and which one is the shortest?

Homework

Write the five family names of Activity 2 in your notebook.

Conclusion

We can name family members and tell who is old or young, tall or short.
