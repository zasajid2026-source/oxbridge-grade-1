Lesson Plan

4.4 — Human body parts
Unit 4 — Me and my family · Book page 37

Learning Objectives

  • Know about the members of a family.
  • Identify the similarities and differences of people.
  • Identify the names of some common body parts.

Introduction

Point to your head, hand and foot. Ask: Our body has many parts. Can you name them?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book: We are human beings. Our body is made up of different parts.
  • Say: These parts have different jobs, but they function together. So we can eat, sit, sleep and play with our friends.
  • Teach the labelled parts from the book's diagram: head, eye, ear, nose, teeth, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, finger, leg, knee, foot, elbow.
  • Share the book's 'Do you know?': The distance between your wrist and your elbow is about the same length as your foot.

Board Work

Body parts: head, eye, ear, nose, teeth, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, finger, elbow, leg, knee, foot.

Class Activity

Children play 'point and name': the teacher names a body part from the book's diagram and children point to it on their own body.

Assessment

  • Name four parts of the body.
  • Why can we eat, sit, sleep and play?

Homework

Draw a person and label any six body parts.

Conclusion

Our body is made of different parts. The parts have different jobs but work together.
