Lesson Plan

2.3 — Activities 1–2 — Parts and needs of plants
Unit 2 — World of plants · Book page 16

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the things that a plant need to grow.
  • Name the parts of a plant.
  • Uses of plants.

Introduction

Ask: What did we learn about the parts of a plant and what plants need?

Teaching Steps

  • Open the book at Activity 1. Children name the parts of the plants in the pictures.
  • Answer the book's two questions with the class: Do both plants have stems? Do both plants have fruits?
  • Move to Activity 2: Look at some plants around the school. What do you notice about the plants? What do plants need?
  • Children write the needs of the plant in the given picture: sunlight, air, water and soil.

Board Work

Activity 1: name the parts. Do both plants have stems? Do both plants have fruits? Activity 2: write what plants need.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activities 1 and 2. Children name plant parts, answer the two questions about stems and fruits, look at plants around the school, and write the needs of the plant in the picture.

Assessment

  • Do all plants have fruits?
  • What does the plant in the picture need?

Homework

Look at a plant at home. Write two of its parts and two things it needs.

Conclusion

In these activities we name the parts of plants and write what plants need to grow.
