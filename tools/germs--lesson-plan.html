Lesson Plan

5.2 — Germs
Unit 5 — Our health · Book page 43

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why we should care for our body.
  • Show how we should do to keep ourselves healthy.
  • Identify the things which can harm our body.

Introduction

Ask: Why do we wash our hands before we eat? What makes us sick?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book's definition: Tiny living things which can harm our body are called germs. They can make us sick.
  • Say: Germs are present everywhere.
  • Say: Flies and dust make our food dirty.
  • Say: Always take fresh and healthy food. We can remain safe from germs by keeping ourselves clean. Then do Activity 1: write the healthy activities in the circles.

Board Work

Germs: tiny living things; can harm our body; make us sick; everywhere; flies and dust make food dirty.

Class Activity

This lesson includes the book's Activity 1. Children write in the circles the activities that will keep them healthy, like washing hands and taking a bath.

Assessment

  • What are germs?
  • What makes our food dirty?

Homework

Write two activities that keep you safe from germs.

Conclusion

Germs are tiny living things that can harm our body. We stay safe by keeping ourselves clean.
