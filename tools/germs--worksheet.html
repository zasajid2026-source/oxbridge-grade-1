Short Questions

1. Why should we keep ourselves clean?

Answer: Germs are present everywhere and can harm our body and make us sick. We remain safe from germs by keeping ourselves clean.

2. Name two activities that will keep you healthy, for Activity 1.

Answer: Any two healthy activities, for example: wash your hands and take a bath daily (also brush your teeth, drink plenty of water).
