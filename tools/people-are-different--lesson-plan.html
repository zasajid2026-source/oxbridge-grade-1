Lesson Plan

4.2 — People are different
Unit 4 — Me and my family · Book page 35

Learning Objectives

  • Know about the members of a family.
  • Identify the similarities and differences of people.
  • Identify the names of some common body parts.

Introduction

Look at two children in class. Ask: Are they exactly the same?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book: People have many things different from each other.
  • List the differences: age, height, weight, skin colours, hair colours, eye colours and gender.
  • Do the book's Activity 1: Look at Ayesha's family picture and spot the similarities among her family members.
  • Say in simple words: We are different in many ways, and we can also be similar in some ways.

Board Work

People differ in: age, height, weight, skin colour, hair colour, eye colour, gender.

Class Activity

This lesson includes the book's Activity 1. Children look at Ayesha's family picture and spot the similarities among her family members.

Assessment

  • Name three ways people are different.
  • What do you spot in Activity 1?

Homework

Write three ways in which you and your friend are different.

Conclusion

People differ in age, height, weight, skin colour, hair colour, eye colour and gender.
