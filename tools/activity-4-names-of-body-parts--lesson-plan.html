Lesson Plan

4.5 — Activity 4 — Write the names of different parts of the body
Unit 4 — Me and my family · Book page 38

Learning Objectives

  • Know about the members of a family.
  • Identify the similarities and differences of people.
  • Identify the names of some common body parts.

Introduction

Ask: Yesterday we saw the labelled diagram of the body. Today we write the names ourselves.

Teaching Steps

  • Open the book at Activity 4. Read the task: Write the names of different parts of the body.
  • Revise the parts from the book's diagram: head, eye, ear, nose, teeth, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, finger, elbow, leg, knee, foot.
  • Read the word stock with the class: feature, younger, similarities, gender, together, function.
  • Children write the name of each body part in the book.

Board Work

Activity 4: write the names of the parts of the body. Word stock: feature, younger, similarities, gender, together, function.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activity 4. Children write the names of different parts of the body by the pictures.

Assessment

  • Name three parts of the body you will write.
  • What does Activity 4 ask you to write?

Homework

Write any six body part names at home without looking at the book.

Conclusion

In Activity 4 we write the names of the parts of our body.
