Short Questions

1. What are domestic animals?

Answer: Domestic animals can live with humans. They depend on humans for food, water and shelter. Farm animals are also included.

2. How are domestic animals useful for us?

Answer: They live and help us on the farms. Some of them provide us milk and meat, and they are useful for us in many other ways.
