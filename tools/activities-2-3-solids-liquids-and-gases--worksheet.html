Short Questions

1. Fill the Activity 2 table with the book's examples.

Answer: Solid: door, coins, tools. Liquid: juice, milk, cooking oil. Gas: water vapours, air in a balloon.

2. How do you know if a thing goes in the Solid column?

Answer: Solids are hard and have a fixed shape and volume. If the thing keeps its shape, it is a solid.
