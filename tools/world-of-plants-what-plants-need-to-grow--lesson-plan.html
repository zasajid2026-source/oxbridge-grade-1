Lesson Plan

2.1 — World of plants — what plants need to grow
Unit 2 — World of plants · Book page 14

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the things that a plant need to grow.
  • Name the parts of a plant.
  • Uses of plants.

Introduction

Show a small plant. Ask: Is a plant alive? What does it need to grow?

Teaching Steps

  • Say: Plants are living things.
  • Write the four needs on the board: sunlight, air, water and soil. Plants need all of these to grow.
  • Say: There are different types of plants. Some plants have flowers. They are called flowering plants.
  • Name the book's flowering plants: rose, daisy, sunflower, jasmine, hibiscus and lily. Say: The flowers of some plants become fruits.

Board Work

Plants need: sunlight, air, water, soil. Flowering plants: rose, daisy, sunflower, jasmine, hibiscus, lily.

Class Activity

Children look at a plant in the school ground. They point and say the four things it needs: sunlight, air, water and soil.

Assessment

  • Name two things a plant needs to grow.
  • Name two flowering plants from the book.

Homework

Write the four things plants need to grow. Draw one flowering plant.

Conclusion

Plants are living things. They need sunlight, air, water and soil to grow.
