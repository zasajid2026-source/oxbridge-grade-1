Lesson Plan

5.3 — Activity 2 — How to keep a healthy body
Unit 5 — Our health · Book page 44

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why we should care for our body.
  • Show how we should do to keep ourselves healthy.
  • Identify the things which can harm our body.

Introduction

Ask: To keep a healthy body, what should you know how to do?

Teaching Steps

  • Open the book at Activity 2. Read: To keep a healthy body, you should know how to do these things.
  • Match the words with the class: wash your hands before you eat.
  • Match: drink plenty of water. Brush your teeth twice a day.
  • Match: take a balanced diet daily. Take a bath daily. Go to sleep at the proper time.

Board Work

Activity 2 matches: wash hands — before you eat; drink — plenty of water; brush teeth — twice a day; balanced diet — daily; bath — daily; sleep — at the proper time.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activity 2. Children join each starting word (bath, hands, plenty, teeth, sleep, diet) to its correct ending to make the healthy-body sentences.

Assessment

  • When should you wash your hands?
  • How much water should you drink?

Homework

Write three healthy-body sentences from Activity 2 at home.

Conclusion

Wash your hands before you eat, drink plenty of water, brush your teeth twice a day, eat a balanced diet, bathe daily and sleep on time.
