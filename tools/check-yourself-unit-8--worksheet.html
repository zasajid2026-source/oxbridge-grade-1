Short Questions

1. True or false: Weather forecasts can be made up to one hour only. Give the right answer.

Answer: False. Reasonable forecasts can be made up to seven days in advance.

2. Name three kinds of day from the pictures in the last part of Check Yourself.

Answer: Any three: sunny, cloudy, windy (also stormy, snowy, rainy).
