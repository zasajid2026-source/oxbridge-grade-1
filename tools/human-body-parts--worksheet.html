Short Questions

1. Write the parts labelled in the book's diagram on the head and face.

Answer: Head, eye, ear, nose and teeth (also neck).

2. How do the parts of our body help us every day?

Answer: The parts have different jobs, but they function together. So we can eat, sit, sleep and play with our friends.
