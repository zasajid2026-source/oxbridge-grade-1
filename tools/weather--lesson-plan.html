Lesson Plan
8.1 — Weather
Unit 8 — Weather · Book page 65

Learning Objectives

  • Know about different kinds of weather.
  • Name the symbols of the weather.
  • Understand about the weather forecast.

Introduction

Look out of the window. Ask: Is today sunny, cloudy or windy? That is weather.

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book: Weather is the state of the atmosphere at a given place at any time. It includes heat, cloudiness, dryness, sun, wind and rain.
  • Say: Weather is driven by air pressure, temperature and moisture differences between one place and another.
  • Say: Weather can vary greatly. It largely depends on climate, seasons and various other factors. Daily changes in the weather are due to winds and storms. Seasonal changes are due to the Earth rotating around the sun.
  • Name the elements of weather: temperature, air pressure, cloud formation, humidity, precipitation, sunshine and wind speed.

Board Work

Weather: state of the atmosphere at a place at any time. Elements: temperature, air pressure, cloud formation, humidity, precipitation, sunshine, wind speed.

Class Activity

Children look out of the window and describe today's weather in one line, using the book's words: heat, cloudiness, sun, wind or rain.

Assessment

  • What is weather?
  • Name three elements of weather.

Homework

Write one line about today's weather and two elements of weather.

Conclusion

Weather is the state of the air around us at a place. It changes day by day and season by season.
