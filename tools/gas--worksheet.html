Short Questions

1. Describe a gas.

Answer: Gases do not have a fixed shape and volume. Gases can change their shape easily and can be compressed easily. Water vapours and air in a balloon are examples of gas.

2. What happens in the balloon-and-brick activity?

Answer: Hold the gas balloon in one hand and a brick in the other hand and squeeze them. The balloon changes shape; the brick keeps its shape.
