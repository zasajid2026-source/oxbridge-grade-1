Lesson Plan

1.6 — Activity 4 — A doll and a girl: same or different?
Unit 1 — Living and non-living things · Book page 10

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish between living and non-living things.
  • Identify different kinds of living and non-living things.

Introduction

Show the book's two pictures: a doll and a girl. Ask: They look alike. Are they the same?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book's sentences with the class: The doll is a non-living thing. The doll does not need air to breathe.
  • Read: The girl is a living thing. The girl needs air to breathe.
  • Find what is the same: Both have two legs and two arms.
  • Children fill in the blanks in the book, choosing the right word each time.

Board Work

Same: two legs, two arms. Different: doll — non-living, does not need air. Girl — living, needs air to breathe.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activity 4. Children look at the pictures of a doll and a girl and fill in the blanks to show how they are same or different.

Assessment

  • What is the same about a doll and a girl?
  • What is different about a doll and a girl?

Homework

Draw a doll and a girl. Write one line: The girl is living. The doll is non-living.

Conclusion

A doll and a girl look the same in some ways, but a girl is living and a doll is non-living.
