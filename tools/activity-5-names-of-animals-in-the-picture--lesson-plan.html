Lesson Plan

3.7 — Activity 5 — Write the names of the animals in the picture
Unit 3 — Animal's world · Book page 28

Learning Objectives

  • Explore animals as living things.
  • Distinguish between domestic and wild animals.
  • Identify animals and their babies.

Introduction

Read the book's 'Do you know?' box: An elephant can live for 60 years. Ask: Can you name the animals in the picture?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the elephant fact: The average life span of an elephant is 60 years. The oldest record age for an elephant is 86 years.
  • Open the book at Activity 5. Look at the animals shown in the picture.
  • The class names each animal aloud.
  • Children write the names of the animals shown in the picture.

Board Work

Elephant: average life span 60 years; oldest record 86 years. Activity 5: write the names of the animals in the picture.

Class Activity

This lesson is the book's Activity 5. Children look at the picture and write the names of the animals shown in it.

Assessment

  • What is the average life span of an elephant?
  • What do you write in Activity 5?

Homework

Write the elephant fact in your notebook: An elephant can live for 60 years.

Conclusion

An elephant lives for a long time — 60 years on average, and the oldest record is 86 years.
