Lesson Plan
2.4 — Uses of plants
Unit 2 — World of plants · Book page 17

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the things that a plant need to grow.
  • Name the parts of a plant.
  • Uses of plants.

Introduction

Ask: We eat food from plants. What else do plants give us?

Teaching Steps

  • Read the book's first line: Plants are important.
  • Say: We need plants for food — for drinking and eating.
  • Say: We need plants for cosmetics and medicines.
  • Say: We need plants for making different types of things. We need plants for making furniture.

Board Work

Plants are important. We need plants for: food, cosmetics, medicines, making things, furniture.

Class Activity

Children name one thing in the class that came from plants and say how we use plants for it, using the book's list.

Assessment

  • Name two uses of plants that are not food.
  • Why are plants important?

Homework

Write three uses of plants at home.

Conclusion

Plants are important. We need them for food, cosmetics, medicines, making things and furniture.
